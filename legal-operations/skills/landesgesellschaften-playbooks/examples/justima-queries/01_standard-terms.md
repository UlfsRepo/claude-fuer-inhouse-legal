# Country-fact queries – PB-01 Standard Terms and Conditions

Run these per priority jurisdiction in the country-law tool (e.g. Justima). Record the answer, the source and the retrieval date in the playbook's country annex (section 8). Keep answers to 2–4 lines; the annex is a lookup table, not a memo.

Suggested order of jurisdictions: [COMPANY-SPECIFIC: by subsidiary request volume].

## Block A – Consumer (B2C)

| Row | Query (paste as written) |
|---|---|
| A1 | What are the requirements for validly incorporating standard terms into a B2C contract concluded online and offline in [COUNTRY]? Is an active checkbox required, or is a visible notice with a link sufficient? |
| A2 | Does [COUNTRY] have a statutory list of prohibited or presumptively unfair clauses in consumer standard terms? List the five most relevant for sale of household appliances (warranty limitation, liability exclusion, price adjustment, unilateral amendment, jurisdiction). |
| A3 | Which mandatory consumer-protection rules in [COUNTRY] cannot be contracted out of by standard terms for the sale of goods: statutory warranty/conformity period, withdrawal period for distance sales, rules on commercial guarantees, price-adjustment clauses? |
| A4 | Is there a requirement in [COUNTRY] that consumer contracts, standard terms or pre-contractual information be provided in the local language? What is the consequence of using English only? |
| A5 | Under [COUNTRY] law, can a trader amend standard terms in an existing consumer contract by notice? What notice period and form are required, and is active consent needed? |

## Block B – Business (B2B)

| Row | Query (paste as written) |
|---|---|
| B1 | How does [COUNTRY] law resolve a conflict between the seller's standard terms and the buyer's purchase terms (battle of forms): last-shot rule, knock-out rule, or first-shot rule? Does a written objection by the seller change the result? |
| B2 | Does [COUNTRY] law apply a fairness or reasonableness test to standard terms between businesses? If yes, which clause types are most frequently held invalid? |
| B3 | Does [COUNTRY] law recognise a doctrine of surprising or unexpected clauses, or a transparency requirement, for standard terms in B2B contracts? |
| B4 | Is a simple retention-of-title clause in the seller's standard terms enforceable in [COUNTRY] against the buyer and against the buyer's insolvency administrator? Are extended or all-monies retention-of-title clauses recognised? |
| B5 | What limits does [COUNTRY] law place on limitation or exclusion of liability in B2B standard terms (gross negligence, wilful misconduct, personal injury, cap as multiple of contract value)? |
| B6 | What formal requirements apply in [COUNTRY] to a jurisdiction clause or arbitration clause contained in standard terms in a B2B contract? Is a reference to terms available online sufficient? |
